kaist-cli

A personal, read-only desktop tool for one KAIST student's coursework schedule.

What it does

kaist-cli is a command-line tool that runs only on its developer's own Windows PC. It reads the developer's own course information (assignments, deadlines, notices, exam schedules) and organizes it for personal study use.

  • Google Calendar (scope calendar.app.created): creates one secondary calendar named "KLMS" and adds deadline and exam events to it. It cannot see or change any other calendar.
  • Gmail (scope gmail.readonly, read-only): reads school e-mails that the developer's university mailbox forwards to the developer's own Gmail, to find exam and class-cancellation notices. It never sends, deletes, or modifies e-mail.

The app is not offered to the public. It is used by a single person, its developer.
